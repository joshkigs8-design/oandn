Presence in Every Fit

EST. 2024 • RUPAS BUSINESS PARK, ELDERT

Crafted for the Bold

O & N FITS emerged from the intersection of Kenyan creativity and global streetwear culture. Founded in the heart of Rupas Business Park, Eldoret, we represent more than fashion—we embody a movement.

Our collections—Loungewear, Official Wear, and Casual Wear—are designed for those who refuse to blend in, who demand both comfort and command attention. Every stitch tells a story of ambition, every silhouette speaks to power.

Every piece is custom-made to order, ensuring perfect fit and unique expression for every individual. We don't just make clothes; we craft confidence.

Maureen - Founder & Creative Director

The visionary behind O & N FITS, bringing Kenyan streetwear to the world stage.
